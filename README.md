## What is 30DayChartChallenge?

A daily data visualization challenge held every April, celebrating the art of chart-making. During the challenge each day features a specific chart type or theme—from comparisons and distributions to relationships and storytelling—pushing you to explore new techniques and expand your visualization toolkit.

More information available at the [30DayChartChallenge GitHub](https://github.com/30DayChartChallenge).

## 🛠 Toolkit Description

A look at the R ecosystem I use to transform raw data into insights:

-   **Core:** `tidyverse` (dplyr, ggplot2, tidyr) for data manipulation and visualization.
-   **Typography & Styling:** `showtext` for custom fonts and `ggtext` for markdown-enhanced labels.
-   **Layout:** `patchwork` for multi-plot composition.
